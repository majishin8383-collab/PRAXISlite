About

A minimal emotional regulation tool.

What this is
A small, fast tool for heavy moments.

Praxis Lite is built for the moments when thinking gets hard. It gives you a simple, structured path to reduce intensity and take one small stabilizing step.

  1. Stabilize — lower intensity (60 seconds)
  2. Stop the Urge — interrupt the loop
  3. Move Forward — one small step
  4. Emergency — outside help is allowed
What this is not
Not therapy. Not a substitute for professional care.

Praxis Lite is a self-guided tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If you are in immediate danger or risk of harm, use local emergency services right now.

In the United States, you can call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.

Privacy
Minimal by design.

Praxis Lite runs entirely in your browser. It does not require an account. Some choices may be saved locally on your device (browser storage) to support basic flow behavior.

If you clear site data for this app, those local values will be removed.
